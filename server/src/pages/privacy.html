Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026 · Applies to https://sonsari.in, its store and booking pages, and our WhatsApp services.

In short: we collect only what's needed to run your store or to place your order or booking: mainly your name, mobile number and, when you order, your delivery address. We don't sell your data. Payments go directly to the shop by cash or UPI; we never see your card or bank details. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see How to delete your data).

1. Who we are

Vocal For Local ("we", "us") runs the platform at sonsari.in. It lets local businesses (shops, salons, clinics and others; "merchants") create online store or appointment pages, and lets customers place orders and book appointments, which are sent to the merchant on WhatsApp. For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), we are the data fiduciary for the account and platform data described here. Each merchant is responsible for how they use the order and booking details you send them.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and run merchant stores and booking pages, and customer accounts.
  • To send your order or booking to the merchant, and to let the merchant confirm it or reply to you on WhatsApp.
  • To verify mobile numbers with one-time codes and keep accounts secure.
  • To show businesses near you and to keep listings genuine (store review and approval).
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, fix problems and improve the service.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We process your data based on your consent and for the legitimate uses permitted under the DPDP Act, such as providing a service you asked for. We do not sell personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • The merchant you order from or book with receives your order or booking details (name, mobile, address, items or service, notes) on WhatsApp and in their dashboard.
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp): when you send or receive messages through WhatsApp, including login codes through the WhatsApp Business Platform, WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy also apply.
  • Service providers who host or support the platform for us, under confidentiality obligations: cloud hosting (Google Cloud, servers in our chosen region), Google Maps (address search and maps), and email or SMS providers (login codes).
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and the public.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and store data: while your account is active, then deleted or anonymised within 90 days of a deletion request or account closure, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Order and booking records: for as long as the merchant's account is active, so they can manage their business, and up to 3 years for dispute and legal purposes.
  • One-time codes expire after 10 minutes. Code-request records are kept for a short period for security.
  • Server logs: up to 90 days.

6. Security

We use HTTPS encryption, hashed login sessions and one-time codes, limits on login attempts, access controls, and regular backups. No system is perfectly secure; if a data breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to: access a summary of your personal data and how we process it; correct or update it; delete it (erasure); withdraw consent; and nominate someone to act on your behalf. You can also raise a grievance with us, and then, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India. Merchants can update most of their details themselves in the dashboard; customers can update their name and address under "My account".

8. How to delete your data

  1. Send a WhatsApp message to +91 93530 95481 with the text "Please delete my data", from the mobile number registered with us. This lets us confirm it's you.
  2. Say whether you are a merchant (include your store link) or a customer.
  3. We will confirm and delete your account and personal data within 30 days, except for records we must keep by law, which we'll restrict and delete when no longer required.

Deleting a merchant account removes the store page, products, services, staff details and photos. Order and booking messages already delivered to WhatsApp chats can't be deleted by us; the merchant and customer control those chats in WhatsApp.

9. Children

The service is not meant for children under 18 to use on their own. We do not knowingly collect children's data without verifiable consent from a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the effective date above and, for significant changes, show a notice on the website.
